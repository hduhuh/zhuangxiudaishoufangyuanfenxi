# Decision

For material decisions, produce a compact decision record:

- **Objective:** what outcome is being optimized.
- **Constraints:** budget, time, permissions, legal/compliance, technical and operational boundaries.
- **Options:** realistic alternatives, including “do nothing / defer” when relevant.
- **Evidence:** facts supporting each option; mark assumptions separately.
- **Trade-offs:** upside, downside, opportunity cost, dependencies and second-order effects.
- **Risk:** probability, impact, detectability, mitigation and rollback.
- **Reversibility:** reversible experiment vs. hard-to-reverse commitment.
- **Recommendation:** one recommended option and why.
- **Acceptance criteria:** observable conditions that mean the decision worked.
- **Review trigger:** what new fact or threshold should cause reconsideration.

When the decision is not yet ready because prerequisites are unresolved, route to `grill-me` / `grilling` first.
